What is POLAN

Replace copper access layers with passive fibre.

A Passive Optical LAN uses single-mode fibre, passive optical splitters and a central OLT to serve ONTs at the edge. Floor-level switches and large copper cabling runs are replaced with a compact, low-power architecture that scales to very large buildings.

On large sites such as hospitals this means fewer comms rooms, lower power and cooling, reduced disruption during installation and a network that stays relevant for decades.
