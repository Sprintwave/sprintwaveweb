FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost the holiday park?

For suitable partnership sites, there is no upfront infrastructure cost to the park.

Who pays for the WiFi?

Customers/holiday-home owners can subscribe directly with Sprintwave.

Does the park have to provide technical support?

No. Sprintwave manages customer and technical support.

Is this just shared guest WiFi?

No. The service is designed to give customers their own private WiFi experience rather than relying solely on a conventional shared guest network.

Can customers connect Smart TVs and streaming devices?

Yes, the private-network model is designed specifically to make normal household devices easier to use.

How long do customers have to subscribe?

Packages can range from short stays through to monthly, seasonal and longer-term options depending on the park.

What internet connection do you use?

Depending on the site we may use Starlink, multiple Starlink services, fibre, leased-line, 4G/5G or a combination.

What happens if something goes wrong?

Sprintwave monitors and supports the service and can arrange onsite engineering where required.

Can we try it first?

Yes. Selected parks can request a free proof-of-concept deployment covering a small part of the site.
